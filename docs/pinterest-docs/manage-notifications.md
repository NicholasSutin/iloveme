# Subscribe for updates

## By email

You can manage emails associated with your app at [Manage your app](https://developers.pinterest.com/apps/).

- Select an **active app ID** and provide a contact email in the **Collaborators** section.

### Two types of collaborators

1. **Email Contacts** are notified of changes to your application or to the Pinterest API via email.
2. **Collaborators** are authorized to view your app secret key and to make changes to the application.

- Collaborators must be employees or partners under the owner's account.

> **Note:**
> If you provide an email alias as an email contact, then the email will go to all members of the alias -- even if those members change over time. Notification of changes to your app's status or breaking changes to the Pinterest API will be sent to the emails of the app's owner, collaborators, and all email contacts.

## Notifications

Make sure Business Updates are enabled within your notification settings:

1. Log in to your business account.
2. Go to [Settings](https://www.pinterest.com/settings/).
3. Select **Notifications**.
4. Edit your settings to control what notifications you will receive directly on Pinterest, via email, or by a push notification to your mobile phone or computer.
